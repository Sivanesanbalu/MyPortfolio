About Me

I am an Artificial Intelligence Engineer specializing in designing and building intelligent, production-ready AI applications that solve real-world business problems. My expertise spans Large Language Models (LLMs), AI Agents, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Learning, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), with a strong focus on transforming research into scalable, practical solutions.

I have hands-on experience developing end-to-end AI systems using Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, FastAPI, Docker, and modern cloud technologies. From model development and fine-tuning to deployment and optimization, I enjoy building complete AI products that deliver measurable impact while maintaining high performance, reliability, and scalability.

My work extends beyond traditional machine learning into efficient AI engineering, including model optimization, intelligent automation, AI-powered applications, and sustainable computing. As an IEEE-published researcher, I have also explored energy-efficient AI systems, focusing on reducing computational cost while maintaining high model performance for real-world deployment.

I am passionate about solving complex engineering challenges, continuously learning emerging AI technologies, and building innovative products that combine strong technical foundations with intuitive user experiences. My goal is to contribute to organizations developing next-generation AI systems that create meaningful value at scale.

AI Interviewer - Smart Voice-Based Interview Platform

A cutting-edge AI-first platform designed to simulate real-time, voice-based mock interviews using Large Language Models like GPT and Gemini Pro. This platform provides a realistic interview experience with instant AI-generated feedback, helping users improve their communication and technical skills.

ROVISA – AI Chatbot with Topic Flow & Typing Effect

A conversational AI chatbot designed to simulate human-like interactions using topic continuity and engaging UI features like typing animations. ROVISA helps users get structured responses across various domains in a natural, flowing dialogue style.

  • Implemented topic flow management to maintain context across multi-turn conversations.
  • Integrated OpenAI GPT-based models to generate dynamic and context-aware replies.
  • Designed a user-friendly frontend with animated typing effects for a natural chat experience.

Offline Voice Assistant (CSV + RAG + Qwen + Whisper Compatible)

An intelligent offline voice assistant designed for low-resource environments, capable of understanding user speech, retrieving relevant answers from a CSV-based knowledge base, and responding naturally using advanced AI models.

  • Used Whisper model for accurate offline speech-to-text conversion.
  • Integrated Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to fetch context-specific answers from structured CSV data.
  • Deployed the lightweight Qwen model to generate precise and low-latency responses.
  • Built to run entirely offline—ensuring privacy, low power consumption, and high efficiency in real-world use cases.
