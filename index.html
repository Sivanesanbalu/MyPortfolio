IEEE Publication: Eco-Transformers

Novel runtime self-pruning and activation freezing framework for sustainable deep learning.

IEEE ICNGCS-2025 Sustainable AI DOI: 10.1109/ICNGCS64900.2025.11183217

Eco-Transformers: Carbon-Efficient and Accelerated Inference through Self-Pruning and Activation Freezing in Large Language Models

Sivanesan B, Shriaarthy E, Sangeetha S, Niranjana R S, Rohini R, Vijayalakshmi N
Conference 3rd Int'l Conf. on Next Generation Computing Systems
Venue & Date PSG iTech, Coimbatore • August 21–22, 2025
Evaluated Models Falcon-RW-1B, GPT-Neo-1.3B, OPT-1.3B, GPT2-XL
Key Results 58% CO2 cut • 56% faster inference • Preserved BLEU

Large Language Models require substantial computational energy, leading to high latency and notable carbon footprints. We introduced Eco-Transformers, a novel runtime framework utilizing PyTorch forward hooks to dynamically mask low-magnitude weights and freeze near-zero activations on-the-fly without requiring fine-tuning, retraining, or modifying model parameters. Across standard benchmarks, Eco-Transformers achieved consistent inference acceleration and carbon reduction while maintaining language generation fidelity.
