IEEE ICNGCS 2025 Best Final Year Project Award Sustainable AI

Eco-Transformers: Carbon-Efficient and Accelerated Inference through Self-Pruning and Activation Freezing in LLMs

Sivanesan B, Shriaarthy E, Sangeetha S, Niranjana R S, Rohini R, Vijayalakshmi N
Conference: 3rd International Conference on Next Generation Computing Systems (IEEE ICNGCS 2025)
Venue: PSG iTech, Coimbatore, India | August 2025
Publisher & DOI: IEEE | 10.1109/ICNGCS64900.2025.11183217
Honors: Best Final Year Project Award – Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, KCT

Large Language Models require immense computational power, leading to high latency and heavy carbon emissions. We introduce Eco-Transformers, a novel runtime optimization framework implementing PyTorch forward hooks that dynamically masks low-magnitude weights and freezes near-zero activations at runtime without requiring retraining or modifying model architecture. Evaluated on Falcon-RW-1B, GPT-Neo-1.3B, OPT-1.3B, and GPT2-XL, Eco-Transformers consistently reduced CO₂ emissions by up to 58% and accelerated inference by 56% while preserving accuracy.
