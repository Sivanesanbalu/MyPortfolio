Eco-Transformers Research

Sustainable AI IEEE ICNGCS-2025 Best Final Year Project Award (KCT)

Eco-Transformers: Carbon-Efficient and Accelerated Inference through Self-Pruning and Activation Freezing in LLMs

Large Language Models require immense computational power, leading to high latency and heavy carbon emissions. We introduce Eco-Transformers, a novel runtime optimization framework implementing PyTorch forward hooks that dynamically masks low-magnitude weights and freezes near-zero activations at runtime without requiring retraining or modifying model architecture.
